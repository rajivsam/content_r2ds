I help organizations build and maintain analytical systems around operational business data using a disciplined open-platform framework. The objective is not to rent a software stack; it is to design the statistical architecture, preserve the analytical knowledge that makes the system durable, and maintain model validity as the operating environment evolves.

{: style="text-align: justify"}
### The Open-Platform Advisory Framework

1. Map the operational process and decision cadence to a well-posed statistical problem.
2. Analyze the current datasets, quality issues, and operational constraints.
3. Design a maintainable analytical structure using open, auditable frameworks such as KMDS and tseda.
4. Build reusable feature engineering components that remain inside the client organization.
5. Diagnose drift, concept changes, and structural degradation before they produce bad decisions.
6. Preserve analytical knowledge for repeatable execution and long-term maintenance.

{: style="text-align: justify"}
### Engagement Phases

* **Initial Solution Mapping** — Define the problem, entities, features, and model framing.
* **Drift & Gap Analysis** — Evaluate whether the underlying assumptions still hold as the data-generating process changes.
* **Remediation & Calibration** — Rebuild the feature definitions, decomposition windows, and model assumptions when necessary.
* **Mathematical Maintenance** — Sustain the analytical system after initial deployment.

{: style="text-align: justify"}
### Step 1: Understand the Data

Questions:

* What is the unit of analysis?
* What entities exist?
* What temporal structure exists?
* What is the baseline data quality?
* Where are the drift signals likely to appear?

Tools:

* Time-series diagnostics
* Dataset profiling
* Feature lineage review

{: style="text-align: justify"}
### Step 2: Understand the Business Process

Questions:

* What decisions are being made?
* What relationships matter?
* What patterns are stable?
* What assumptions are economically valuable to preserve?

Tools:

* KMDS component analysis
* Operational process mapping
* Statistical dependency review

{: style="text-align: justify"}
### Step 3: Build Analytical Assets

Questions:

* What features matter?
* What models are appropriate?
* What operational assumptions need to be explicit?
* What is the maintenance plan when drift emerges?

Tools:

* Featurization
* Modeling governance
* Open-source analytical implementation

{: style="text-align: justify"}
### Step 4: Preserve and Maintain the Mathematical System

Questions:

* What was learned?
* What assumptions were made?
* How is the solution maintained over time?
* Where is the model at risk of drifting from physical reality?

Tools:

* KMDS
* tseda
* Drift analysis and calibration protocols

{: style="text-align: justify"}
### Typical Engagement Outcomes

* A clear analytical architecture for the business problem
* Reusable and auditable feature definitions
* Explicit model assumptions and operational limits
* Drift diagnosis and remediation plans
* Durable knowledge ownership inside the client organization
